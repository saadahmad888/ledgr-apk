Everything stays on your phone

Ledgr works with no internet and no account. Your spending, notes and receipts are saved only on this device.

Log a spend in three taps

Type the amount, pick a category like Grocery, Fuel or Salon, and save. Attach a photo of the receipt when you have one.

Notes you can speak or tick off

Write plain notes, or make a checklist for shopping. Tap the mic and speak — your words are typed for you.

Save towards something real

Set a goal with a target amount and a date. Add money whenever you can and watch the ring fill up.

Set it up

Three quick things. All of them can be changed later.

Your country also decides the payment options you'll see when recording a spend — UPI, Pix, M-Pesa, Zelle, iDEAL and the rest.
