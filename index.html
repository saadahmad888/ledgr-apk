Installing the APK

  1. Tap Download the APK above. Your browser may warn that this file type can be harmful — that message appears for every APK, so continue.
  2. Open the downloaded file from your notifications or the Downloads folder.
  3. Android will ask permission to install from this source. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. Ledgr appears in your app drawer.
Play Protect may show a "scan this app" prompt because the file did not come from the Play Store. Choosing either option is fine — the app installs the same way.

What it does

  • 💸
    Expenses in three taps — amount, category, save. Grocery, fuel, salon, bills and a dozen more, plus your own.
  • 🧾
    Receipt photos attached to any transaction, shrunk so they stay small.
  • 🌍
    Your country's money — 125 countries, 90-plus currencies, and local payment methods like UPI, Easypaisa, Pix, M-Pesa and Zelle.
  • 📝
    Notes and checklists you can dictate instead of typing.
  • 🎯
    Savings goals with a target, a date and a daily pace.
  • 📤
    Export to PDF, Excel or CSV, and a full backup file that restores everything.
  • 🔒
    No account, no server — everything sits in the app's storage on your phone.
